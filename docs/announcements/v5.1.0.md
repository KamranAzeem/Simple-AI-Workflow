# Simple-AI-Workflow v5.1.0

This release gives your design documents their own trigger, so you can write them down while the design is still fresh in your mind.

## What changed

Until now, the design-document flow was a set of rules with no single command to run. You can now say:

    "update design documents"

and add what it is about, like:

    "update design documents related to the payment gateway app"

The AI finds the right documents, proposes each change, challenges the design decisions with a few sharp questions, and shows you a diff before it writes anything. Your design documents stay clean: design only, no commit hashes, no session notes, no work logs.

A few related changes shipped alongside:

- A separate trigger, `"migrate design docs"`, moves your documents from `ai/shared/project-knowledge/` into `design-docs/` beside your code. It lists every file and shows the git impact before it moves anything. It is the only command that moves documents between locations.
- If you want to record where your design documents live, ask the AI to add a `## Design Docs` section to your `ai-customization.md`, or write it yourself with `**Location**: design-docs/`.
- The Delivery Ledger now updates when a design document changes, not at every checkpoint. The checkpoint never touches your design documents.
- After a conversation is compacted, the AI reloads your design documents along with your knowledge, so it knows which designs exist.

## Upgrading

Nothing breaks if you do nothing. The new triggers are additive. If your design documents still live under `ai/shared/project-knowledge/`, they keep working there, and the AI offers to migrate when it helps.

The full release notes are in the [v5.1.0 release](https://github.com/KamranAzeem/Simple-AI-Workflow/releases/tag/v5.1.0).
