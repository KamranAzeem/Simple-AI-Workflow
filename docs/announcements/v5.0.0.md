# Simple-AI-Workflow v5.0.0

This release moves your design documents out of the hidden `ai/` folder and puts them beside your code, where they belong.

## What changed

Design documents, the vision, PRD, HLD, LLD (with acceptance criteria), risk and gap analysis, ADRs, and the Delivery Ledger, now live in a `design-docs/` directory next to the code, not under `ai/`.

Why: the design of a solution is part of the solution. It should be versioned with the code, visible to anyone who collaborates with you, and still there in six months when you fix a defect or add a feature. The `ai/` folder is the AI's private working area for tickets, plans, and state. It is often git-ignored and may never be backed up, so it is the wrong home for something this durable.

Where the design documents live now:

- A single repository keeps them in `design-docs/` at the project root.
- A project root that holds several repositories keeps the solution-level documents and a rollup in the project-root `design-docs/`, and each component's documents in that component's own `design-docs/`.
- The Delivery Ledger lives in `design-docs/` as well.

A few related refinements shipped with this release:

- The document chain is notes, vision, PRD, HLD, LLD (with acceptance criteria), risk and gap analysis, ADRs, and delivery ledger.
- Risk and gap analysis is a tracked artifact.
- The depth is proportional: a small change needs context and acceptance criteria, while medium, large, and architectural work gets the full set.
- ADRs hold genuine design decisions only. They are never a changelog or a command log.
- The AI now indexes every `design-docs/` directory at startup, so it knows what exists without reading it all.

## Upgrading

Nothing breaks if you do nothing. The AI now looks in `design-docs/`, and your existing documents under `ai/shared/project-knowledge/` remain where they are and are still indexed.

If you want to move them, say:

    "migrate design docs"

The AI moves them into `design-docs/` with your approval and shows the git impact. This is optional, and you lose nothing by leaving them where they are.
