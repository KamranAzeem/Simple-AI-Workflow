# v7.5.0

Every Critical, Major, and Minor review finding now blocks approval. One gate, owned by the code-review policy, covers every review: findings are fixed before `APPROVED`. Suggestion stays optional, the reviewer stays read-only, and there is no cap or demotion.

- The issue policy, the plan and implementation procedures, the handoff and repair procedures, the design, knowledge, and global-settings policies, and the workflow guide all cite the gate.
- The gate defines the diff scope, the reviewed-file scope, and the ticket scope. A finding inside any scope blocks. A valid finding outside the ticket scope but inside the diff or reviewed-file scope defers only with your explicit approval.
- The tests and the ADR store are updated. The protocol counts stay at 25 policies, 16 procedures, and 54 triggers.
