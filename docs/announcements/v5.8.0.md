# Simple AI Workflow v5.8.0

Prepared 2026-11-09; published with the final approval.

## Policies can load by phrase

Say `"cloud standards"`, `"data standards"`, `"database standards"`, or `"backend standards"` and the matching policy loads for the session, without adding it to `## Active Expertise`.

- At most one optional `## Triggers` section per policy, one phrase per bullet.
- The trigger list is rebuilt at load context, post-compaction recovery, and checkpoint re-affirmation; it is never written to disk.
- A policy loaded only by a trigger is session-scoped. It does not survive a compaction; say the phrase again.
- `"use policy <name>"` loads any policy by its Active Expertise name.
- A question, statement, past-tense mention, hypothetical, or negation does not run a trigger; an ambiguous phrase asks first.
- Duplicate or overlapping trigger phrases across modules are rejected by the validator.

## Upgrade

Pull the latest workflow, run the sync script, and restart the session. No customization change is needed.
