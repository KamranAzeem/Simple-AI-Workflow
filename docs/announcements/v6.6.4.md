# Simple AI Workflow v6.6.4

Released 2026-11-18.

## The common policy now reads like a rulebook

`ai-policy-common.md` is the file every session loads. Before the plugin split it had grown to 869 lines. The split brought it to 465. The rules were still long paragraphs, and many of them carried the reason inside the rule. A reader had to untangle the action from the explanation.

I rewrote it. Every rule is one sentence in one bullet. The reasons moved out. The structure is the same, and no rule changed.

## What stayed

- The canonical state-file headers are byte-identical.
- The state-file ownership protocol is the same.
- The sections and the test anchors are the same.
- The `.gitconfig` template is the same.

## What changed

- Every multi-sentence rule is now one sentence.
- The rationale bullets left the policy. Their reasons are recorded so nothing is lost.
- The vague modal words are gone.
- The prose sections are flat bullets.
- The design invariants are untouched.

## The guard

`tests/test-common-prose-shape.sh` checks the file:

- No banned modal word.
- One sentence per item.
- No prose outside a rule.
- The compatibility anchors.

It passes on the new file. It fails on the old one with 132 findings, so the shape cannot come back silently.

## Checks

The protocol validator passes 8 of 8. The authoring validator passes. All 21 test scripts pass. The four canonical header hashes match. The file is 682 lines, 6,934 words, and 44,857 bytes, about 2.3 KB smaller than before.

## Upgrade

Pull the workflow, run the sync script, and restart the session. No rule changed, so nothing else needs doing.
